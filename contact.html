Common questions

Honest answers before you ask.

Do I have to commit to a certain number of months?

No. Coaching is billed monthly and you can join or cancel any time. No minimum term. To stay with a team during a race must be apart of the team for at least three months prior or prepay for three months.

Do I have to attend every team race?

No. Race weekends are opt-in per event — pay the flat room fee only for races you actually go to.

What if the team grows too big for one house?

We will try to accomodate as many people as possible in the available houses. First come first serve for booking your spot in the house.

What kit is for me?

Check out the Races and Kit section for more information, but as a beginner that doesn't want to invest as much the standard kit is the best option. If you are looking to get faster then I recommend going for the higher model. Take a look at varlo's website for the kit details. If you are just interested in running, the our run singlets are your best bet. We would love for our athletes all to race as one team.

Are you certified?

We are currently certified as a USA Triathlon Associate certification, which includes SafeSport, a background check, and coaching liability insurance. As we grow so will our certifications, we have had experience with being certified coaches in the past
