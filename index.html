Our methodology

We coach with your data, not a guess.

The same approach that took us from below-average to professional racing — applied to every athlete on the roster.

What we track

  • Power-to-heart-rate-to-cadence relationships, tracked over the season to catch fatigue and fitness shifts early
  • Training load related to sleep and recovery stats week to week — not just whether a single workout hit a number but if the body is absorbing the work.
  • Catching red flags in your data before they manifest as performance issues
  • Pulled directly from your Garmin, Coros, Apple, or Strava history

What it means for your plan

  • Your plan is individually adjusted — not a fixed template handed to the whole roster
  • Check-ins and messages are based on what your numbers actually show, not a mandatory regulated call.
  • Issues get caught in the data before they show up as a bad race
  • Personable human coaches that you are able to tell anything to with quick feedback and advice.

How we coach

Your data is not just a nubmer, but a story.

Every plan is built and adjusted from your training, health, and life stats — not a generic chart handed to everyone on the roster.
